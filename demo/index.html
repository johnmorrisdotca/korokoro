Korokoroコロコロ

Fair dice for the table, with the odds of every throw. Pick up to ten dice, from a d4 to a d100, and tap the felt to roll.

GitHub npm

More dice, by notation

The buttons are the dice a table owns: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d30 and d100. Anything else is typed into the notation box beside the bonus, or tapped here.

Rolls use your device's cryptographic generator unless you choose a seed. Your history stays on this device. Dice sounds from Kenney's Casino Audio, CC0. pnpm add @johnmorrisdotca/korokoro · MIT © John Morris
